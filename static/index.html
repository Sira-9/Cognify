Cognitive Load Modeling

How Cognify Personalizes Your Pacing

Cognify uses a non-invasive behavioral prediction engine to estimate your current learning load on specific concepts.

LOW COGNITIVE LOAD

High confidence, rapid answers, no hints. The engine advances faster, accelerates curriculum, and provides harder challenge questions.

MEDIUM COGNITIVE LOAD (Desirable Difficulty)

Optimal zone of proximal development. Normal pace and balanced practice questions to reinforce long-term memory.

HIGH COGNITIVE LOAD (Mental Friction)

Multiple attempts, hesitation, hints requested. The engine slows down, reduces question difficulty to 'Easy', provides concrete analogies and step-by-step worked examples.

Important Pedagogical Principle: Cognitive load is an estimate of temporary mental effort for this specific concept, NOT an evaluation of your intelligence or permanent ability.
